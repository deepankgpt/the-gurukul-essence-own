Healing Modalities

Personalized Yoga Therapy

Each session is crafted with ancient techniques tailored to your individual needs — not a class, a conversation with your own body.

🌬️

Pranayama & Breathwork

Ancient breathing techniques — Anulom Vilom, Kapalbhati, Bhramari — to purify the nadis, calm the mind, and strengthen the vital life force (prana).

🧠

Mental Health & Mindfulness

Guided practices for stress, anxiety, and emotional balance using yogic meditation, dharana, and pratyahara — reconnecting you to inner stillness.

🌿

Physical Health & Pain Relief

Therapeutic asana sequences addressing chronic pain, spine issues, joint stiffness, and postural imbalances through gentle, root-cause healing.

🌙

Yoga Nidra & Deep Rest

The ancient science of yogic sleep — a deeply restorative practice that heals the nervous system, dissolves stress patterns, and restores energy.

🏢

Corporate Wellness

On-site and online wellness sessions for organizations — covering breathwork, mindfulness, and movement to boost focus, morale, and team wellbeing.

☀️

Lifestyle & Holistic Balance

Personalized programs aligning sleep, digestion, energy, and daily rhythm through yogic living — grounded in Ayurveda and Patanjali's teachings.
